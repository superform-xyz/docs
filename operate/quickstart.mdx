---
title: "Launch sequence"
description: "Prepare a vault without confusing deployment, execution readiness, and listing."
---

<Steps>
<Step title="Create or select the vault">
Open **Operate → My SuperVaults** and verify the chain and address. For a new vault, use **Create a SuperVault** at `/operate/create`. Review identity, managers, fees, and metadata; sign only the intended deployment, then wait for indexing. [Create a vault](/operate/ui/create-vault) describes the transaction and recovery path.
</Step>
<Step title="Prepare sources and pricing">
In **Yield Sources**, select the intended source and compatible oracle, review asset/share units and pricing, then complete the whitelist transaction. A source must exist before hook parameters and strategy plans can refer to it. See [Yield sources and pricing](/operate/ui/yield-sources).
</Step>
<Step title="Authorize hooks">
Open **Hooks**. Save and activate the intended configuration version, generate its tree, and compare it with the current onchain root. The Primary Manager proposes the strategy root; after the configured timelock, execute the update and verify it. Publish the matching proof artifact separately. See [Hooks and authorization](/operate/ui/merkle-trees).
</Step>
<Step title="Prepare execution and funding">
Check the executor's manager status and session validity in [Execution accounts](/operate/ui/execution-accounts). Fund [UP upkeep and native gas](/operate/ui/funding) independently. A hook count, strategy count, or active flag does not prove an executable plan.
</Step>
<Step title="Save and test an inactive strategy">
Follow [Build your first strategy](/operate/strategy/quickstart). Read back the saved definition, version, and inactive lifecycle state. Check expressions, units, and plan fixtures; use [Simulation](/operate/ui/simulation) only for admitted inputs and modeled actions. Do not start production automation to test an editor.
</Step>
<Step title="Review launch evidence">
Use the [Launch checklist](/operate/ui/setup-checklist) to reconcile deployment, authorization, execution readiness, and distribution. Activation of an approved strategy and public listing are separate decisions. Private testing does not require a public listing. Monitor actual runs through [Intent History](/operate/ui/intent-history) and [Positions and accounting](/operate/ui/dashboard).
</Step>
</Steps>
