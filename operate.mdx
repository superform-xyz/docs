## Operating boundaries

Strategy rules decide **when** the offchain engine may publish an intent. Onchain manager checks, hook registration, Merkle proofs, and venue constraints determine **what** a transaction can execute. A disclosed rule is not automatically an onchain restriction.

A saved definition, a published intent, a completed transaction, and the intended economic outcome are separate evidence. See [Concepts](/operate/concepts) for the enforcement layers, [Launch checklist](/operate/ui/setup-checklist) for readiness, and [Playbooks](/operate/playbooks/carry) for portfolio operations.
