Superform v2 contracts are deployed with deterministic addresses. Unless a table says otherwise, an address is identical on every chain listed in its **Chains** column. Contracts whose address differs per chain, or that exist on a single chain, are listed under [Chain-Specific Addresses](#chain-specific-addresses).

<Note>
This page is generated from the production deployment outputs of the `v2-core`, `v2-periphery` and `stx-contracts` repositories (last synced 2026-11-24). Hook and oracle addresses change when new versions are deployed, so always verify against the onchain registry before integrating directly.
</Note>
