## Wallet Requirements

SuperformOS uses [Dynamic.xyz](https://dynamic.xyz) for wallet/account sign-in. Follow [Authentication](/operate/authentication) to establish your browser session.

- **EVM-compatible wallet** (MetaMask, Rabby, Frame, or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet)
- **Native gas token** for each chain where you operate vaults
- **Operator access** assigned on at least one vault before SuperformOS features are accessible

<Note>
View-only access is assigned offchain; private vault and rule inspection still requires an authenticated account with access to that vault. Writes require the role authorized for that operation.
</Note>

## Getting Access

**Joining an existing vault:** The Primary Manager assigns Secondary Managers through an onchain transaction. Either manager can add or remove view-only collaborators and update their display names offchain through [User Management](/operate/ui/user-management).

**Creating a new vault:** Any wallet with operator access can create a vault and automatically becomes its primary manager.
